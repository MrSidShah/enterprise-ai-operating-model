# About This Repository

This repository represents a working knowledge base on enterprise AI — how to design it, govern it, operationalise it, and extract measurable commercial value from it across large and complex organisations.

The frameworks here are written from direct experience leading data, AI and analytics functions at scale — across global operations, federated business structures, and multi-market environments spanning media, entertainment, and professional services.

The goal is to bridge the gap between executive AI strategy and practical implementation. Most organisations have one or the other. Very few have both working together.

---

## Who This Is For

- Chief Data Officers and Chief AI Officers designing enterprise AI strategies
- Senior data and analytics leaders building or scaling AI platforms
- Architects and engineers implementing enterprise AI systems
- Executives evaluating AI operating models and governance approaches

---

## Lens and Perspective

The frameworks in this repository are written with a specific lens:

- **Scale over simplicity** — designed for organisations with complex, distributed environments, not greenfield startups
- **Commercial first** — every framework anchors back to measurable business outcomes
- **Practitioner perspective** — written by someone who has run these programmes, not just advised on them
- **Evergreen over trending** — principles that hold across model generations and vendor cycles
